# CS230-Operating-Platfiorms
Briefly summarize The Gaming Room client and their software requirements. Who was the client? What type of software did they want you to design?
The client, The Gaming Room, was looking to expand their pre-existing platform for the Draw It or Lose It game. The app already existed on Android platforms and they were looking to expand to a web-based version to facilitate more players over a cross-platform design. Each game needed to be unique, facilitating one or more teams with multiple players, all with unique team names as to not create duplicate teams or games. They wanted the web-based version to look and feel similar to the already established Android app so that people who currently played would be familiar with the software and could immediately jump into games.

How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your software design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
It's important to consider the user's needs and wants because ultimately you want to impress them with your product so they come back for future projects. Being able to clearly convey their needs into a successful product ensures you have a happy client and a efficient program you can bring back to them.

A lot of research will help a long way. I think even though it takes some extra time, having the necessary research will overall help with designing software and ensuring you have all the right tools at your disposal.
